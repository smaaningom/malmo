Where Sweden actually needs workers

Twice a year, Arbetsförmedlingen (the Swedish public employment service) rates every occupation's recruitment situation — shortage, balance, or surplus — plus a 5-year demand forecast. This page is that data, sorted so the biggest shortages come first. No spin, nothing scraped or guessed.

Source: Arbetsförmedlingen, Yrkesbarometer, round 2026-1 (public domain).
