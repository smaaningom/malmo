Mjukvaru- och systemutvecklare

Software and systems developers

Education

Most job ads ask for a university degree in computer science or software engineering (3 years, "högskoleingenjör", or 5 years, "civilingenjör"). In practice, plenty of working developers got in a different way: a 1–2 year "YH" (yrkeshögskola) vocational IT program, a foreign CS degree, a coding bootcamp, or a strong portfolio/GitHub with no formal degree at all — especially for junior and mid-level roles. Formal education matters more the more senior or specialized the role (e.g. embedded systems, security).

Language

Not strictly required at a lot of companies. Many Swedish tech workplaces — international companies, startups, most of Stockholm/ Gothenburg/Malmö's tech scene — run day-to-day work in English. Job ads often list "svenska" as a plus or don't mention it at all; some do genuinely require it (public sector, anything client-facing in Swedish, smaller traditional companies). Read the actual ad, not just the assumption that Swedish is mandatory — this is one of the occupations where it usually isn't.

Other things worth knowing

  • No license or certification is required to work as a developer (unlike e.g. electricians or nurses) — hiring is portfolio/ interview-based.
  • A personnummer (personal identity number) and BankID are needed once you're employed (payroll, bank account, signing your contract) — not before or during interviews.
  • A public GitHub, past projects, or a technical take-home test typically matter more to hiring managers than the degree itself.
  • This is a national shortage occupation with rising 5-year demand (see the status above) — one of the stronger bets on this list right now.

Why only one occupation: the shortage/surplus data on the main page comes from Arbetsförmedlingen's Yrkesbarometer, a clean published dataset. Education/language/requirement info like this page isn't in that dataset — it doesn't exist as one systematic source anywhere yet. Doing this properly for all 205 occupations means either analyzing real job-ad text at scale (Arbetsförmedlingen's JobSearch API has structured fields for this) or writing each one by hand. Neither is done yet — this page is a mockup of the shape, not a promise it's built.
