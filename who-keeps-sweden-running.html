Who keeps Sweden running

In Swedish municipalities and regions, foreign-born staff went from 13% to 22% of employees between 2014 and 2024 (municipalities), and 14% to almost 20% (regions). Some occupations rely on foreign-born workers far more than that average — here they are, next to whether Sweden currently has a shortage in that job.

Source: SKR (Sweden's municipal/regional employers' association), "Personalen i välfärden 2024" — public sector only, not the whole economy. Shortage status: Arbetsförmedlingen, Yrkesbarometer, round 2026-1.
